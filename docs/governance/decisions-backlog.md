# Decisions Backlog

Running log of pending maintenance tasks, open questions, and issues found during design system work.

## How to use this file

When you or an AI agent discovers a gap, inconsistency, or deferred decision:

1. Add an entry to **Open** below.
2. Do not silently work around missing guidelines — record the issue here.
3. Move items to **Resolved** when addressed, with a link to the fix or doc.

## Entry format

```markdown
### [YYYY-MM-DD] Short title

- **Category:** foundation | component | pattern | governance | tooling
- **Severity:** low | medium | high
- **Description:** What is missing or wrong?
- **Suggested action:** What should happen next?
- **Owner:** (optional)
```
